# 🌌 LifeSolver Product Vision & Core Verticals

## 1. Vision
LifeSolver is an omniscient Personal Operating System (Life OS) consolidating daily planning, deep focus, second brain learning, personal finance, health routines, asset safeguarding, network nurture, and AI intelligence into a cohesive command center.

## 2. The 8 Core Verticals
1. **Planner**: Tasks, Hierarchical Subtasks, Kanban, Calendar Time-Blocking, Goals & Milestones.
2. **Mind**: Markdown Knowledge Base, Bookmarks, Reading List, SM-2 Spaced Repetition Flashcards, Mood Journal.
3. **Finance**: Dual Cash Ledgers (Standard & Special), Spending Budgets, Savings Goals, Recurring Bills, Isolated Loan Tracker.
4. **Health**: Habits & Streaks, Gym Workouts (Sets/Reps/Logs), Minimalist Vitals Scorecard (Water, Protein, Sleep).
5. **Focus**: Pomodoro, Deep Work Timers, Chrome Extension Screen Time Sync, Feed Eradicator, Dopamine Detox.
6. **Assets**: Physical Inventory, Equipment Servicing, Warranty Reminders, Zero-Knowledge AES-GCM-256 Vault.
7. **Network**: Personal CRM, Contacts, Important Dates (Birthdays, Anniversaries), Commitments & Reminders.
8. **Copilot**: Orbit AI Omniscient Engine, Natural Language Multi-Intent Entry, Action Preview & Approval Cards.
